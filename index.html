Questions, answered

Is Bloom a menopause HRT tracker?

Yes — Bloom is built for menopause and perimenopause HRT. It's ready for oestrogen (estradiol) patches, gels, tablets and pellets, plus progesterone and testosterone, and its daily check-in tracks menopause symptoms like hot flushes, night sweats, sleep, mood, anxiety and brain fog.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your own device, in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and no one else can see it.

Does it work offline?

Yes — once installed, Bloom works fully offline. Your calendar, doses and journal are always available.

Which HRT methods are supported?

Oestrogen patches, pills/tablets, gels & creams, and implants/pellets — plus progesterone and testosterone — with flexible schedules from daily to every 12 weeks, so twice-weekly patches or cyclical HRT are easy to plan. You can also set fully custom planned dates.

Will I lose my data if I clear my browser?

Clearing site data in your browser will erase entries, so avoid "clear browsing data" for this site. Installing it as an app helps keep it safe.
