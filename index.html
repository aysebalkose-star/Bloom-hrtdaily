Questions, answered

Is HRT Daily a menopause HRT tracker?

Yes — HRT Daily is built for menopause and perimenopause HRT, also called menopausal hormone therapy (MHT) in the US. It's ready for estrogen (oestrogen / estradiol) patches, gels, tablets and pellets, plus progesterone and testosterone, and its daily check-in tracks symptoms like hot flashes (hot flushes), night sweats, sleep, mood, anxiety and brain fog. Read the full guide to tracking menopause HRT →

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your own device, in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and no one else can see it.

Does it work offline?

Yes — once installed, HRT Daily works fully offline. Your calendar, doses and journal are always available.

Which HRT methods are supported?

Estrogen (oestrogen) patches, pills/tablets, gels & creams, and implants/pellets — plus progesterone and testosterone — with flexible schedules from daily to every 12 weeks, so twice-weekly patches or cyclical HRT are easy to plan. You can also set fully custom planned dates.

Will I lose my data if I clear my browser?

Clearing site data in your browser will erase entries, so avoid "clear browsing data" for this site. Installing it as an app helps keep it safe.
