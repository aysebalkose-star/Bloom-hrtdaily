How to keep track of your HRT doses (a simple daily system)

Did you take tonight's progesterone? Was today a patch day, or did you just mean to change it? If you've ever stood there genuinely unsure whether you've taken your hormones, you're in good company — and the fix isn't trying harder to remember. It's a small system that remembers for you. Here's how to keep track of your HRT intake without it taking over your life.

A quick note: this is general information, not medical advice. If you've missed doses or you're unsure what to do, your pharmacist or prescriber can help — never hesitate to ask.

Why HRT is so easy to lose track of

It isn't you. Taking hormones rarely has a single, fixed ritual the way a morning coffee does — a patch sits quietly for days, a gel goes on at a slightly different time each morning, a capsule competes with everything else at bedtime. Different treatments on different schedules, landing on different days of the week, are genuinely hard for any brain to hold — and menopause itself can turn down the mental "sticky note" that used to catch these details. Keeping track isn't about discipline. It's about having somewhere reliable for the information to live.

What "keeping track" really means

Good tracking answers three questions, instantly, whenever you wonder:

  • What's due today? — which treatments to take or change, right now.
  • Did I already take it? — so you never double up or skip by accident.
  • Is it working? — a sense, over weeks, of whether your symptoms are easing.

You don't need to log blood pressure or spreadsheets. You need those three answers to be one glance away.

A simple system that works

The most reliable approach has three small parts:

  • Give each treatment a fixed rhythm. Wherever your prescriber allows it, pin twice-weekly patches to set weekdays (say Monday and Thursday) and take daily doses at the same moment each day. A rhythm is far easier to keep than a rolling "every few days".
  • Attach it to something you already do. Change the patch with your Monday coffee; take progesterone when you brush your teeth at night. Anchoring a new habit to an old one is the single most effective trick there is.
  • Log it the moment you do it. One tap, one tick, one mark — immediately, not "later". That log is what answers "did I already take it?" at 7am tomorrow.

Ways to track your intake

Any of these beats relying on memory — pick the one you'll actually keep up:

  • Write the date on the patch — a tiny number in biro answers "when did I put this on?" instantly.
  • A paper calendar or diary — tick each dose off. Simple and visible, though easy to forget to carry.
  • A pillbox — great for daily tablets, less so for patches, gels and varied schedules.
  • A dedicated HRT tracker — the tidiest option: it shows what's due, logs an intake in a tap, plans your whole month, and keeps a record of how you feel. That's exactly what we built HRT Daily to do.

The best method is simply the one that's always with you and takes seconds — because a system you abandon in a week doesn't keep you on track.

If you lose track or miss one

First, don't panic — a missed or uncertain dose is common and usually not a crisis. What to do next depends on your specific treatment, so follow the advice that came with it or ask your pharmacist, who can tell you in a minute whether to carry on as normal. Then let that be the nudge to set up a system, so the next "did I?" moment simply has an answer.

Common questions

How do I keep track of my HRT doses?

Give each treatment a fixed rhythm (for example, patches on set weekdays and daily doses at the same time), attach it to something you already do like your morning coffee or brushing your teeth, and log each dose the moment you take it. A dedicated HRT tracker makes this easiest by showing what is due and recording each intake in a tap.

What is the best way to track HRT?

The best method is the one you will actually keep up. Options include writing the date on a patch, ticking a paper calendar, a pillbox for daily tablets, or a dedicated HRT tracker app. A tracker tends to be tidiest because it handles every method and schedule, shows what is due at a glance, and keeps a record of how you feel.

How do I remember to take my HRT every day?

Anchor it to an existing daily habit so it happens automatically, take it at the same time each day, and log it immediately so you can always check whether you have already taken it. Keeping a visible record, rather than relying on memory, is what stops the daily guessing.

What should I do if I lose track of whether I took my HRT?

A missed or uncertain dose is common and usually not an emergency, but the right next step depends on your specific treatment, so follow its instructions or ask your pharmacist. Then set up a simple system or tracker so the next time you wonder, the answer is one glance away.
