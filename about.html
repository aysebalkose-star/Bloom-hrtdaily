About HRT Daily

HRT Daily exists for one quiet, everyday problem: keeping track of your hormone intake when life is busy and menopause has turned the volume down on the part of your brain that holds small, repeating details.

Why we built a hormone-intake tracker

Hormone replacement therapy sounds simple until you're living it. A patch twice a week, a capsule each night, a pump of gel each morning — layered over work, family and everything else. It's so easy to stand in the bathroom and genuinely not know: did I change the patch on Monday, or just mean to? Have I taken tonight's progesterone? Is any of this even working yet?

That uncertainty is exhausting, and it's not a failing on your part — it's a system problem. We built HRT Daily to move the remembering out of your head and into something you can trust, so keeping track of what you've taken, and what's due, takes seconds instead of worry.

What HRT Daily does

  • Tracks every intake — patches, pills, gels, creams and pellets, on any schedule, logged in a single tap.
  • Plans ahead — tap the dates you take each treatment and the calendar remembers, so you never count days again.
  • Shows the whole month — every treatment in its own colour, logged, planned and due at a glance.
  • Notices how you feel — a quiet daily journal, so over a few weeks you can see whether your HRT is working.

Private by design

Your hormone intake is some of the most personal information there is, so we built HRT Daily to keep it that way. Everything you enter stays on your own device — there's no account, nothing is uploaded to a server, and it works fully offline. We can't see your data, and neither can anyone else. You can read exactly how we handle the little that a purchase involves in our Privacy Notice.

Who's behind it

HRT Daily is a small, independent project — built by people who needed a calmer way to keep on top of their own HRT and couldn't find one that was simple, private and made for menopause rather than periods. We're not a big health company, and we're not medical professionals. What we are is careful: every feature is there to make tracking your intake easier, and nothing more.

Because of that, HRT Daily is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device, and it doesn't give medical advice. The decisions about your treatment belong with you and your prescriber — HRT Daily just helps you keep an accurate record and walk into your reviews with real information.
