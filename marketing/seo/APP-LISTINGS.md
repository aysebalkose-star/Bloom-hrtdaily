## 9) Submission playbook

1. **Be consistent.** Same name, same URL (`https://hrtdaily.com`), same logo, same descriptions everywhere. Inconsistency dilutes the entity signal.
2. **Stagger it.** 2–4 submissions a day, not 40 in one afternoon — a sudden burst of identical links looks spammy.
3. **Lead with the privacy angle.** "No account, offline, all on your device" is your genuine differentiator — most trackers are the opposite.
4. **Use real screenshots** (`icon-512.png` + the 4 screens). Directories reject thin/blank listings.
5. **Save the live listing URLs** in the tracker below — you'll want them for later (and some directories let you add a reciprocal link or update details).
6. **Launch big ones on the right day.** Product Hunt: Tue–Thu, 12:01am PT. Line up a few friends to be early supporters (genuine, not fake).
7. **Never fake reviews or use multiple accounts** — it gets listings removed and can harm the brand.
